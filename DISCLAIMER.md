## Assumption of Risk

By using PHG, you acknowledge and agree that:

1. **You are responsible for your own health decisions.** Any action you take based on information organized in or generated from your Personal Health Graph is taken at your own risk and discretion.

2. **AI-generated analysis is not clinical-grade.** Outputs produced by AI models reading PHG files — including risk assessments, supplement recommendations, genetic interpretations, pattern analyses, and clinical correlations — are informational only. They have not been validated through clinical trials, peer review, or regulatory approval. They may contain errors, omissions, or inaccuracies.

3. **Genetic information has inherent limitations.** Consumer genotyping arrays test a limited subset of known variants. The absence of a variant in a raw genotype file means it was not tested, not that you do not carry it. Genetic interpretations based on consumer data should be confirmed by clinical-grade testing before informing medical decisions. Polygenic risk scores from consumer platforms are not validated for clinical decision-making.

4. **Lab result interpretation requires clinical context.** Biomarker values organized in PHG are presented without the full clinical context that a treating physician would consider. Reference ranges vary by laboratory, methodology, patient demographics, and clinical circumstances. An out-of-range value does not necessarily indicate disease, and a within-range value does not guarantee health.

5. **Supplement and medication decisions require professional guidance.** PHG skills may generate analyses that evaluate supplements or flag potential drug-gene interactions. These analyses are informational and must not be used as the sole basis for starting, stopping, adjusting, or substituting any supplement, medication, or treatment. Pharmacogenomic information is complex and should be interpreted by a qualified pharmacist, genetic counselor, or physician.

6. **Risk scores are estimates, not predictions.** Clinical risk calculators referenced in PHG skills (ASCVD Pooled Cohort Equations, Framingham Risk Score, Reynolds Risk Score, etc.) produce population-level probability estimates. They do not predict individual outcomes. Their accuracy varies by demographic group, and they may over- or under-estimate risk for specific populations.

- When you provide PHG files to an AI model (cloud-based or local), your health data is processed by that model's infrastructure and is subject to that provider's privacy policy and terms of service.
- When you connect external services via MCP connectors, your data may be transmitted to those services.

**You are solely responsible for the security and privacy of your health data.** Review the privacy policies of any AI service or third-party tool before sharing your health information with it.
