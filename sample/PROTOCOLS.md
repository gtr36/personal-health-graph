# Protocols

## Morning Routine
- **Wake time:** 6:45am weekdays, ~7:30am weekends
- **Sunlight exposure:** 10-minute walk after waking, most days
- **Supplements:** See SUPPLEMENTS.md (morning stack)
- **Caffeine timing:** Two coffees. First ~7:15am. Second was often 2-3pm until June 2026; since 2026-06-29 the rule is no caffeine after 12pm (see EXPERIMENTS.md)
- **Other habits:** Lisinopril with breakfast

## Evening Routine
- **Screen cutoff:** Target 10:00pm, actual closer to 10:45pm
- **Supplements:** See SUPPLEMENTS.md (evening stack); atorvastatin taken with them
- **Sleep target:** 7.5 hours (actual has averaged ~6.9 per WHOOP; better since late June 2026)
- **Room conditions:** 66°F, blackout curtains, no white noise

## Exercise

### Strength Training
- **Frequency:** 3x/week (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **Split/program:** Upper / lower / full body, simple progressive overload
- **Duration:** ~45 minutes
- **Tracking method:** WHOOP strain plus a notes app log
- **Current focus:** Consistency over intensity

### Cardio
- **Type:** Zone 2 stationary cycling
- **Frequency:** 2x/week (Tue/Sat)
- **Duration:** 40 minutes
- **Target HR zone:** 115-130 bpm
- **Tracking method:** WHOOP

### Recovery
- **Rest days:** Thursday, Sunday
- **Active recovery:** Dog walks daily
- **Other:** None regular

## Nutrition
- **Framework:** Loosely Mediterranean; cooking most weeknights
- **Protein target:** ~140g/day, hit maybe 5 days out of 7
- **Meal timing:** Breakfast 7am, lunch 12:30pm, dinner 7pm
- **Meal frequency:** 3 meals, one afternoon snack
- **Alcohol:** 2-3 drinks/week, usually weekend
- **Hydration:** ~2.5L/day; more in summer (Denver is dry)
- **Restrictions/allergies:** None

## Sleep
- **Target duration:** 7.5 hours
- **Consistent bedtime:** ~10:45-11:15pm
- **Consistent wake time:** 6:45am weekdays
- **Sleep environment:** Cool, dark, phone charges across the room (since May 2026)
- **Known issues:** Slow to wind down; sleep latency ran ~28 minutes before the June 2026 caffeine change, ~16 minutes after
- **Tracking:** WHOOP 4.0

## Supplement Cycling
- Nothing cycled. Vitamin D3 is continuous with retest at each lab panel.

## Other Interventions
- Breathwork app a few nights per week during stressful stretches; inconsistent.
