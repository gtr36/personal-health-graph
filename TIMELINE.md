<!--
HOW TO USE THIS FILE:
This is the derived chronological spine of your health graph — every dated event from every
file, flattened onto one timeline. It exists because health data is asynchronous: labs arrive
quarterly, wearable data daily, symptoms sporadically, medication changes on no schedule at
all. Correlation and causality questions are chronology questions, and this file is the index
